FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Everything clear before downloading.

Do I need to run any script on my laptop every time?

No! With v1.1 Instant Extension Mode, Chrome itself hosts the WebRTC bridge and displays the QR code right in the extension popup. And if you also install the optional Windows Agent via install-silent-startup.bat, it starts invisibly with Windows so you never touch a script again.

Do I have to scan the QR code every day?

No. Once you scan the QR code once and add the PWA to your phone's home screen, your encrypted pairing key is remembered—opening the app reconnects to your Chrome browser automatically.

Is my connection private and encrypted?

Yes. WebRTC DataChannels are end-to-end encrypted via DTLS/SCTP directly between your phone and your browser, guarded by your unique 96-bit pairing key or PIN.

Does «Screen Spy» consume CPU when closed?

Zero percent. Tab or desktop capture stays completely idle until you actively toggle «Screen Spy» on your phone.
